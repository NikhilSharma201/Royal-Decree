BY ORDER OF THE COURT COUNCIL

A Royal Decree Concerning
the Wellbeing of Her Majesty

Sovereign of the Wards, Keeper of the Textbooks, Ruler of the Exam Hall

Let it be known throughout the realm that the Council has convened in urgent session, having received troubling reports from the border provinces (namely, Your Majesty's group chat) that the Crown has been overworking itself in service of the Great Examinations. The Council, though composed of but one loyal and lowly commoner, hereby issues the following Articles for Her Majesty's compliance.

  1. ARTICLE I

    On the Matter of Water

    Her Majesty shall drink water at intervals throughout the day, and not only when fainting seems imminent. The court physicians have made their concerns known.

  2. ARTICLE II

    The Curfew Clause

    Sleep is not a reward to be earned after the syllabus is finished...it is owed to the Crown regardless. Four hours is a nap, not a night.

  3. ARTICLE III

    On Proper Sustenance

    A real meal, eaten sitting down, counts as royal duty. Biscuits and stress do not constitute a balanced diet, whatever the exam schedule may suggest.

  4. ARTICLE IV

    The Fresh Air Provision

    Her Majesty is hereby ordered to leave the desk at least once per day for reasons unrelated to studying. Even the castle walls need a change of view!

  5. ARTICLE V

    On the Nature of Worth

    No single exam, however important it feels tonight, determines the Crown's value. Her Majesty was formidable before this syllabus and shall remain so after it.

  6. ARTICLE VI

    Standing Availability of the Peasant

    The undersigned commoner remains on call at all hours for venting, distraction, or silence, and requires no reply, no scheduling, and no formality whatsoever.(Although he might fall asleep...)

Let it be recorded that the realm believes in her, exams included.

Drafted and sealed with great affection, A LOYAL COMMONER OF THE COURT
